### 🗣️ Speaking Part 2 + Part 3 (20 นาที — จับเวลาด้วย)

**Part 2 — Cue Card (เตรียม 1 นาที / พูดสด 2 นาที)**

> Describe a subscription or paid membership you have that you feel is, or is not, worth the money.
>
> You should say:
> - what it is and how you started paying for it
> - how often you use it
> - what you get from it
>
> and explain whether you would keep paying for it if the price doubled.

**Part 3 — ถกประเด็นต่อยอด**
1. Why do many companies prefer to charge a monthly subscription rather than a one-time price?
2. Do you think free services funded by advertising are really free for the people who use them?
3. Should governments make it easier for consumers to cancel subscriptions, even if this hurts companies' profits?
