### Model Essay (~300 คำ — เทียบหลังเขียนเสร็จเท่านั้น)

A growing number of online platforms now ask customers to pay a monthly fee instead of funding their services entirely through advertisements. This essay will explain why businesses favour this approach and argue that it benefits consumers only when they remain in control of what they pay for.

There are two main reasons why companies prefer subscriptions. The first is predictability. Advertising income rises and falls with the economy and with the budgets of a relatively small number of advertisers, whereas recurring revenue from millions of small monthly payments allows a firm to forecast its income and plan long-term investment with greater confidence. The second reason is customer retention. A subscriber who has stored years of files, playlists or settings on one platform faces a form of lock-in, so leaving becomes inconvenient, and this keeps churn low without the firm having to win that customer's attention every day.

In my view, this development is good for consumers only to a limited extent. On the positive side, people who pay directly usually enjoy a cleaner experience without constant interruptions, and the company has a stronger incentive to serve them rather than advertisers. However, the model also has a serious drawback. Small monthly charges are easy to ignore, and many households end up paying for several services they rarely use. For example, an office worker on a modest salary could easily spend more each year on forgotten memberships than on a one-off purchase that would have lasted for years. This is why subscription fatigue has become such a common complaint.

In conclusion, firms are moving towards subscriptions because they provide stable income and make customers less likely to leave. Although users may receive a better service as a result, the trend only works in their favour if they regularly review their spending and can cancel without difficulty.
