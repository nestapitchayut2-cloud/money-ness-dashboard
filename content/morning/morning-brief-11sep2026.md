> Many salaried workers keep most of their savings sitting in a low-interest bank account, believing this is the safest way to protect their money.
>
> **What problems does this behaviour cause, and what solutions could help people make better use of their savings?**

## 👁️ เฉลย (ห้ามดูก่อนทำเสร็จ)

### Model Essay (~290 คำ)

Many working professionals choose to leave the bulk of their salary sitting in a savings account, convinced that this is the most prudent way to protect what they have earned. While this instinct is understandable, it creates several problems, and fortunately, there are practical solutions that can help people put their money to better use.

The first major problem is the erosion of purchasing power over time. Savings accounts in most countries pay minimal interest, often well below the rate of inflation, meaning that money left untouched effectively loses value every year even though the account balance stays the same. A second problem is the opportunity cost involved: cash sitting idle earns nothing, whereas the same amount invested in assets such as index funds or government bonds could generate steady returns and compound significantly over decades. For a salaried employee saving for retirement, choosing the "safe" option can therefore end up being the more expensive one in the long run.

Several solutions can address these issues. First, financial literacy programmes provided by employers or governments could teach workers the basics of asset allocation, helping them understand that a diversified portfolio, rather than cash alone, is what truly protects long-term wealth. Second, automatic contribution schemes — similar to provident funds — could default a portion of every paycheque into low-cost, diversified investment funds unless the employee actively opts out, nudging people toward productive saving without requiring active decision-making. Both measures target the same root cause: a lack of awareness of the real, invisible cost of standing still.

In conclusion, keeping money entirely in cash may feel safe, but it quietly costs workers through inflation and lost investment returns. Combining financial education with default investment mechanisms would allow people to keep the security they want while avoiding the silent cost of doing nothing with their money.

### เฉลย Vocab Review
1. opportunity cost
2. real interest rate
3. hedge
4. non-yielding asset
5. cost of carry
